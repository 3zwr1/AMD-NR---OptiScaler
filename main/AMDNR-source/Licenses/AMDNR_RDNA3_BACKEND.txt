What this covers
----------------

The RDNA 3 (gfx11) backend for lmxxf's network: lmxxf's HIP kernels built for RDNA 3
with lmxxf_gfx11_compat.h, which carries their RDNA 4 FP8 matrix work on RDNA 3's F16
matrix units (verified against the RDNA 4 result), and the RDNA 3 HIP modules built
from it - HIP/gfx1100, HIP/gfx1101, HIP/gfx1102 and HIP/gfx1151 inside
LmxxfNrRuntime.pak.

Where the credit lives
----------------------

  * Every RDNA 3 module embeds the credit line above; LmxxfNrRuntime.dll refuses an
    RDNA 3 module that does not carry it.
  * LmxxfNrRuntime.pak (format v2) starts with a plaintext notice that names lmxxf and
    this backend. The notice is authenticated with the pack's table: a pack whose
    notice was edited or removed is refused by the runtime.
  * LmxxfNrRuntime.dll's version resource (Properties > Details) carries the copyright.
