1. What this notice covers
--------------------------

The following is AMDNR's own work, Copyright (c) 2026 3zwr1 (AMDNR):

  * every source file, shader and script written for AMDNR;
  * AMDNR's modifications to OptiScaler's files and to the other upstream code
    listed in section 5;
  * the release texts, menus and documentation written for AMDNR.

It covers every AMDNR release from 0.1.0 onward, including 0.3.3, 0.3.4, 0.3.5,
0.3.5.2, 0.3.5.3 and later, as recorded in CHANGELOG.md. Highlights:

  * AMDNR's AMD Neural Rendering pipeline: model interleave (Guided fill, Edit
    accumulation and the self-tuning interleave), the temporal stability pass,
    the residual controls (strength, limit, edge fade) with the Catmull-Rom
    resample and its ringing bound on the upstream residual resolve, NR
    resolution above 100% and dynamic NR resolution (the 25-100% NR resolution
    is upstream), the detail/colour and sharpen passes, the highlight proxy and
    its decode by the original, NR style presets, the HOME toggle default with
    its notice and input fix (the ToggleKey binding is upstream), the NR capture
    tool and the Neural tab.
  * The lmxxf runtime inside OptiScaler (0.3.0 and later): AMDNR's integration of
    lmxxf's network into OptiScaler's AMD path, the LmxxfNrRuntime.pak format,
    network history and passes, the edit shaper, auto-exposure, self-healing,
    the Vulkan-bridge warm-up, and the UE5 queue and command-list robustness.
  * The lmxxf RDNA 3 (RX 7000) backend (0.3.3) and its handheld builds for
    gfx1103 and gfx1150 (0.3.4); see AMDNR_RDNA3_BACKEND.txt.
  * The "c32w" one-wave C32 kernels for the lmxxf network on RDNA 4 (0.3.3.2;
    amdnr_c32w_family.hsaco in LmxxfNrRuntime.pak, SHA-256 pinned in
    LmxxfNrRuntime.dll), Copyright (c) 2026 3zwr1 (AMDNR). They run lmxxf's
    network (Kien, MIT; see lmxxf_LICENSE.txt). Acknowledgement: AMD's public
    RDNA 4 WMMA documentation (AMD GPUOpen, the ROCm matrix instruction
    calculator), used for ideas only; no code was taken from it.
  * AMDNR Anywhere (0.3.5): the capture-host mode of OptiScaler.dll - host
    detection, the Anywhere tab and its host settings, the host input path, the
    carry rules for a captured window, and the placement of the neural pass
    after upscaling ([DlssNr] AmdPlacement). The capture host itself is not
    part of this work (see section 5).
  * The Ray Regeneration tab, its backend and noise rules, the frame-generation
    tab rules (FgRestartNotice, FgPairRules, FgSwapchainReuse), the resize
    rules, the adapter and HIP-device rules, the shared runtime folder, the
    anti-cheat process rule, the mod-loader and ReShade detection (0.3.5).
  * The Anywhere host's output, edit, exposure and pacing rules, the style slot
    rules, the handheld highlight ceiling and pass rule, the game frame-
    generation provider rule, the HIP start-up evidence and the FSR 4 (INT8)
    handheld opt-in rules (0.3.5); and the loading of lmxxf 0.37's and 0.39's
    modules as a pinned group with its per-GPU defaults, set choice, off
    switches and fallbacks (the kernels and their launch code are lmxxf's, see
    section 5).
  * The 0.3.5 RDNA 3 module set of LmxxfNrRuntime.pak (the g2 operand-assembly
    build of lmxxf's kernels for gfx1100 / gfx1101 / gfx1102 / gfx1103): lmxxf's
    kernels (Kien, MIT), built by AMDNR with its own recipe; AMDNR claims the
    build, not the kernels.
  * The lmxxf network size tier policy (the tier snap and its RDNA 3 default),
    the small 360p / 576p network sizes, the per-GPU gating of handheld APUs,
    and the native character mask controls for lmxxf (0.3.4).
  * AMDNR's ports to lmxxf (0.3.4): Network output, Encoding, Residual edge
    fade, the Screen-space GI host, interleave pacing and the Game exposure
    switch; and for danielblnc's runtime: the runtime settings (Network style,
    Tone curve, Black lift, Game exposure), the highlight colour guard and the
    edit shaper modes.
  * The AMDNR menu of 0.3.4 (the rebuilt Neural tab and the shared look of every
    tab), Save report, the crash log, and the menu input fixes.
  * The integration of RenoDX's colour composition into both AMD runtimes (0.3.3;
    the maths is RenoDX's and the dlssnr.hlsl lineage's - Dagherbou, wilsjo2 - see
    RenoDX_ATTRIBUTION.txt and the CREDITS in amd/NrCompose.h).
  * The FSR Ray Regeneration path-traced profile, the SSS-guided skin smoothing
    and the Ray Regeneration A/B, tuning and diagnostic tools (0.3.3 - 0.3.4).
  * The graphics-state hooks and restore (GraphicsSnapshotHooks.h) on the
    upstream snapshot bookkeeping (GraphicsSnapshot.h); not installed in 0.3.3.
  * AMDNR Screen-space GI by 3zwr1 (dlssnr/gi; 0.3.4 preview, official since
    0.3.5.3): screen-space ambient occlusion and bounced light, its shaders,
    host, menu section, rules and tests. Written clean-room from published
    research only; no code from the inherited Screen-space GI or any other
    project. Ideas from: Therrien, Levesque and Gilet 2023 (visibility
    bitmask screen-space indirect lighting); Jimenez, Wu, Pesce and Jarabo
    2016 (GTAO, its slice integral and multi-bounce fit); Jimenez 2014
    (interleaved gradient noise); Roberts (the R2 sequence); Schied et al.
    2017 (SVGF) and Schied, Peters and Dachsbacher 2018 (gradient-guided
    temporal filtering); Dammertz et al. 2010 (edge-avoiding a-trous); Kopf
    et al. 2007 (joint bilateral upsampling); Karis 2014 and Salvi 2016
    (history clamping); Turanszki 2019 and Wu 2020 (normals from depth);
    Mayaux 2018 (HBIL: near-field methods need a far field); Hartley 1997
    (rotating-camera self-calibration); IEC 61966-2-1 (the sRGB transfer
    function). AMDNR's own: the far field's surplus-over-ambient rule, the
    planar-connectivity joins, the ambient level and the AO share.
  * The 0.3.5.2 rules and their suites: the driver-reset backoff and its session
    ceiling (NrTimeoutRules.h), who carries the capture host's upscale and the
    history reset that lets FSR 4 take it with Neural Rendering on

    rule applied where an exposure value enters a dispatch (ExposureRules.h),
    the neural cost ceiling (NrCostRules.h), the ENB detection rules, the HIP
    start-up cause line, and the per-game entries AMDNR added to the quirk

    GPL-3.0 as the rest of it, and are described in section 5.
  * The 0.3.5.3 work and its suites: the AMDNR Ray Denoiser (FSRDArd*,
    FSRDGpuTimer, RrArdMenuRules.h; clean-room record in
    shaders/fsrd_preprocess/ARD-CLEANROOM.md - ideas from Schied et al. 2017
    (SVGF), Dammertz et al. 2010 (edge-avoiding a-trous), Karis 2014 and Salvi
    2016 (history rectification), Cigolle et al. 2014 (octahedral encoding));
    the Ray Regeneration hit-distance and quality rules (FSRDHitDistanceRules.h,
    FSRDQualityRules.h); Ray Reconstruction in Vulkan titles and the bridge key
    rules (VkRrRules.h, VkQueueFamilyRules.h, VkBridgeKeysRules.h,
    Dx11BridgeKeysRules.h); the NVIDIA look (NrLookRules.h); the lmxxf runtime's
    own 2560x1440 and native network sizes (native_size_rule.h,
    native_size_stages.h, native_tier_policy.h and their device queries); the
